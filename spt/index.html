Data format & acquisition checklist

Choose up to 12 UTF-8 text files. CSV, TSV and text-based .spt files are accepted; binary files need conversion first. The size limit applies to all selected files together.

Describe the track ID, frame or timestamp, and position columns. Include position units, pixel size if coordinates are in pixels, time between frames, exposure time, missing frames, and experimental conditions. Say which values are unknown.

For an immediate spatial preview, use CSV or TSV columns track_id, t_s, x_um, y_um. Here t_s is elapsed time in seconds and x_um, y_um are positions in micrometers. Convert values before renaming columns; frame numbers and pixel coordinates need their acquisition calibration. Without t_s, only spatial paths are previewed. Automatic preview is limited to CSV/TSV files up to 16 MiB. Other formats or larger accepted files can be inspected and normalized by the agent. Example: “My columns are particle, frame, x, y; positions are pixels, 100 nm/pixel, 20 ms/frame. Check tracking gaps and localization noise before fitting.”
